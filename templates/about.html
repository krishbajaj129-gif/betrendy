About BeTrendy

BeTrendy shows which clothing styles are rising or fading, based on what people search for in India. By turning search interest into clear velocity scores and 8-week predictive trajectories, it helps you spot the next big look before it hits the mainstream.

How the Numbers Work

1

We collect search interest from Google Trends

We gather search interest across key men's and women's fashion categories in India directly through Google Trends queries.

2

We compare the last few weeks with the weeks before

To avoid daily spikes or temporary noise, our algorithm calculates the average interest over a recent window and compares it directly against the prior baseline window of equal duration.

3

We label each item Rising, Stable or Declining

Items with a growth rate of +15% or higher are classified as Rising, items dropping by -15% or worse are marked Declining, and those in between are marked Stable.

📌

Scores are relative (0 to 100), not search counts. Predictions are estimates based on past interest, not guarantees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the data come from? +
The search interest numbers are fetched from Google Trends for India. Google Trends normalizes search queries against overall search volume within the selected geography and timeframe, generating an indexed score from 0 to 100 where 100 represents peak interest.
Why does it say "Showing sample data"? +
Google Trends frequently applies strict HTTP 429 rate limits to automated API calls. To guarantee that BeTrendy always loads smoothly, rapidly, and reliably during academic presentations and testing, the application automatically falls back to an offline curated 52-week dataset whenever live rate limits are encountered, or when the Sample Data toggle is switched on.
How accurate are the predictions? +
Our forecasting engine uses an explainable scikit-learn Ordinary Least Squares Linear Regression model fitted to the past 26 weeks of search momentum to project an 8-week forward slope. While it mathematically models ongoing trend velocity, fashion demand is also influenced by seasonal changes, viral pop-culture, and unexpected events; therefore, predictions are statistical estimates, not guarantees.
Is my password safe? +
Yes, your password is safe. Passwords are never stored in plain text anywhere in the database or server logs. When you register, Werkzeug's cryptographic hashing function generates a salted, one-way hash (scrypt/pbkdf2:sha256). When you log in, your input is hashed and compared to the stored hash—meaning nobody, not even the database administrator, can read your actual password.
